About Us

Ayurveda You Can Trust, For Every Stage

At Doctors Ayurcare, we practise classical Ayurveda across the full range of therapies — Abhyangam, Kizhi, Nasya, Kati Vasthi — for pain, stress, fatigue, and everyday wellness. Every plan is designed and reviewed by our doctor, not handed off to a fixed protocol.

Within that, postpartum recovery is where we go deepest. The weeks after childbirth shape a woman's health for years, and our Prasava Raksha program gives new mothers a dedicated, week-by-week recovery plan that most clinics don't offer.
